LAB / Privacy

A simple site. A small footprint.

The site itself does not use analytics, advertising pixels, account systems or embedded social feeds.

Settings stay in your browser

Theme and language preferences may be saved in localStorage. They are not sent to this site or shared with a third party. Search runs in your browser; it does not send search terms to a search service. Search terms may be included in the page address.

External sites and hosting

YouTube and research-publisher links open external websites with their own policies. No YouTube player is loaded automatically. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs such as IP address and requested page. That is separate from the site code.

Offline use

Articles, charts, search and bundled downloads work from the extracted folder. Videos and external publications need an internet connection. This is a description of this version of the site, not a claim about every future hosting configuration.
